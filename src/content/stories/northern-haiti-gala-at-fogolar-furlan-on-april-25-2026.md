Join us for an unforgettable evening at the Javelin Fund Gala on Saturday, April 25, 2026, at the Fogolar Furlan Club in Windsor, Ontario.

Doors open at 5:30 PM, with dinner starting at 6:30 PM.

Tickets are $100, with a special student price of $75.

For tickets, call Maggie at 519-890-8240.

This event is made possible by Families First, and will feature a showcase of Haitian entertainment and art.

Hosted by CTV's Bob Bellacicco, the evening will also include door prizes, including a wheelbarrow of wine.

Don't miss out on this incredible opportunity to support the Javelin Fund and enjoy a night of culture and community.
