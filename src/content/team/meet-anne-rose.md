Anne Rose is an elderly woman living in the village of Deppé. She was about to totally lose her vision because of glaucoma. When she got diagnosed with the condition of glaucoma, her children and her were very desperate because they don't work and have no money, no resource to pay for the expensive treatment.

Then, we asked for help from the "Javelin Foundation" to assist her, and it was the best thing that can happens to her.

The Javelin Foundation has fully paid all the treatment, surgery, procedures, glasses she has been needing for her recovery since more than 5 years until now.

Her vision has improved a lot.

The first time she saw the ophthalmologist, the eyes pressure was almost 30mmHg and now with all the medicines she is using, the eye's pressure is getting normal, and the vision improved.

Last time, it was 12mmHg. She can see much better, the doctors are very satisfied with all the improvement she is getting. She is more than happy and also her family.

It has been possible thanks to the help of the Javelin Foundation; you are people of good heart and full of compassion. The eyes are one of the most important organs in the human body and help someone who was about to totally lose her vision to get it back is a blessing. She can walk in the village by herself with nobody to guide her.

Also, she receives an allowance that help her to survive and take care of herself. Her life has been changed since the Javelin has responded "yes" to help her. She is more joyful; she has more energy and feel much better. She has no more depression.
