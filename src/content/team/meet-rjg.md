Center de Refuge des Orphelins d'Haïti will present to you today our great supporter which is the Javelin organization Education and Fund. We are thanking him for everything he has done for us and that allows us to continue to exist as a Reception Center that allows us to receive small people to help them. After 2 years, we also find it necessary to thank the Javelin Education and Fund organization that provides the orphanage Center de Refuge des Orphelins d'Haïti with a lot of support that allows us to continue operating. All these support help us to have an NGO status and patent as well. We are saying a big thank you to Mr. GLEN JACKSON for the follow-up he always does every month to keep the orphanage happy. It is thanks to Mr. GLEN that our children go to school, they are taken care of and he helps us build space for the little ones to sleep as well. We will never stop thanking Mr. Gilen, we will continue to pray for him to find more strength and courage to continue helping the little people.
