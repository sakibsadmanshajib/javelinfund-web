The CRDEH, which is the Centre de Refuge et de Développement des Enfants d'Haïti had this testimonial. We are thanking the fund for everything it has done for us and that allows us to continue to exist as a Reception Center that allows us to receive small people (orphans) to help them.

After 2 years, we also find it necessary to thank the Javelin Education and Medical Fund organization that provides the orphanage Center de Refuge des Orphelins d'Haïti with a lot of support that allows us to continue operating. All these support help us to have an NGO status and patent as well.

We are saying a big thank you to GLEN JACKSON for the follow-up he always does every month to keep the orphanage happy. It is thanks to Mr. GLEN that our children go to school, they are taken care of, get medical care when needed  and he helps us build space for the little ones to sleep as well.

We will never stop thanking Mr. Glen, we will continue to pray for him to find more strength and courage to continue helping the little people.
