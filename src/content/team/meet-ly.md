I live in Cap Haitien (Haiti), I met Mr Glen Jackson, in 2018 at that time I was living in extreme poverty, I had trouble financing my studies, rent, struggling to pay for medical care, I accidentally ate because I am an orphan of mother and father, The Javelin fund allowed Mr Glen to help me finance my studies for 4 years and today thanks to The Javelin fund I have a degree in education sciences, in 2019 I had a serious health problem which required urgent surgery thanks to the Javelin fund I underwent 2 surgeries, I thank the Hearts together for Haiti foundation for everything he did for me via the Javelin fund, Mr Glen is a dad for me personally and for the other people he helps in Haiti. May God bless Mr Glen and the people who support this noble project, and also bless the foundation for the work he does in Haiti. Thank you--

L.Y.
